Visas

For a Swedish passport, in the order you cross each border. Checked 21 October 2026. Rules change at short notice, so recheck each one about a week before you cross.

Summary Thailand Laos Vietnam Cambodia Thailand again Malaysia Singapore Philippines What to carry

The short version

You need no visa bought in advance anywhere on this route. Laos and Cambodia sell you one at the border for US dollars in cash. Everywhere else lets Swedes in free. Most countries now want an online arrival form filled in a few days before you land.

Use only the official sites

Every arrival form below is free on the government site. Lookalike websites charge $50–100 for the same form, and they rank high on Google. The official addresses are listed on each card.

1 · Thailand

Flying into Bangkok from Copenhagen.

ThailandAir · Bangkok Suvarnabhumi
Visa
None needed
Stay
30 days
Cost
Free
You'll stay
~2.5 weeks
  • The visa-free stay was cut from 60 to 30 days on 15 September 2026. Your northern leg is about 14 nights, so it fits.
  • The catch is proof of leaving. Thai embassies say you should be able to show a ticket out of Thailand within 30 days. Your flight home is on 26 Feb, more than 30 days away, so the airline may question you at check-in in Copenhagen.
  • The fix: carry a booking that leaves Thailand within 30 days. A slow-boat package that crosses into Laos works, or any cheap onward flight. Print it or keep it offline.
  • Fill in the TDAC (Thailand Digital Arrival Card) online within 3 days before you land: tdac.immigration.go.th. Free.
  • If you ever need longer, immigration offices can extend a stay by up to 30 days, for about 1,900 baht.
Sources: Thai Embassy Stockholm · VisasNews

2 · Laos

Walking across the Friendship Bridge from Chiang Khong to Huay Xai.

LaosLand · Huay Xai
Visa
On arrival
Stay
30 days
Cost
~US$40 cash
You'll stay
~1 week
  • The visa-free deal for Nordic passports ended on 1 January 2025. Swedes need a visa again.
  • Visa on arrival at the bridge: fill in a form, hand over a passport photo and the fee. Sources give US$30–50, with US$40 the most common figure. Bring clean, untorn US dollars in small notes. There's sometimes a small extra charge at weekends or outside office hours.
  • The alternative is an eVisa (about US$50) from laoevisa.gov.la. Check the list that Huay Xai accepts it. The visa on arrival is simpler and cheaper.
  • Fill in the Lao Digital Immigration Form (LDIF) online before you cross. It's being rolled out at all international checkpoints in 2026.
  • Your passport needs at least 6 months' validity.
Sources: ScandAsia · Visa Traveler · US Commercial Guide

3 · Vietnam

Flying from Luang Prabang to Hanoi.

  • Swedes get 45 days visa-free under Resolution 44, which runs from 15 March 2025 to 14 March 2028. With Sapa included, your Vietnam leg comes to about 24 nights, so it fits with room to spare.
  • The 45 days can't be extended inside Vietnam. If your plan grows past 45 days, apply for an e-visa instead before you go: up to 90 days, US$25 single entry, only at evisa.gov.vn.
  • No arrival form currently.

  • Buy the e-Visa before you go, or get a visa on arrival. Both work at Phnom Penh's airport. The e-Visa is only sold at evisa.gov.kh; lookalike sites charge more.
  • For a visa on arrival, bring US dollars in cash and a passport photo. The official fee is around US$30.
  • Fill in the e-Arrival card online before you fly. It's required at airports.
  • Cambodia runs on US dollars anyway, so carry some small notes for the whole stay.
Sources: Cambodian Embassy, official border list · Exo Travel on e-Arrival

5 · Thailand again

Option 1: flying in from Penang. Option 2: flying in from Siem Reap. Both: Manila → Bangkok at the end for the flight home.

Visa
None needed
Stay

Andaman leg
~2 weeks
Last visit

  • Each entry gives you a fresh 30 days. Thailand limits visa-free entries by land to two a year, but you always arrive by air, so this doesn't affect you.
  • Fill in a new TDAC before each arrival.
  • For the Andaman entry, carry proof that you're leaving: the flight to Manila (Option 1) or the Lipe → Langkawi ferry booking (Option 2).
  • In Option 2 you leave Thailand at Ko Lipe's immigration office before the ferry, so get your exit stamp there.

What to carry for the borders

  • Passport valid until at least September 2027, six months past your return.
  • At least 8 blank pages. The Lao and Cambodian visas each fill a whole page.
  • 4–6 passport photos for visas on arrival.
  • About US$150 in clean, small, untorn notes: both visa fees plus spare. Damaged notes get refused.
  • Offline copies of your passport, onward bookings and insurance.
  • Register on Svensklistan with the Swedish Foreign Ministry, so the embassy can reach you in a crisis.
